# Update Problems

Open the main window's **More > Updates...** menu and read its status. An update needs a compatible published release and a working connection to the download service.

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A check cannot reach the service | Check your connection and try **Check for updates** again. Keep using the installed app while the service is unavailable. |
| No update is offered | Read the check result. A newer compatible published release must be available before downloading is offered. |
| A download fails or is cancelled | Retry from the Updates window. Checking or downloading does not replace the installed app. |
| A download cannot be verified | Keep the installed version and retry through the app. Do not replace individual program files to bypass the error. |
| **Install and restart** is unavailable | Finish or stop playback and recording, and close the current settings dialog. |
| The license dialog is waiting | Read the terms and explicitly accept to install, or choose **Cancel** to stay on the installed version. |
| The workspace cannot be saved | The app stays open. Use Save, resolve the reported problem and retry when the app is idle. |

Save the open workflow and wait for its confirmation before an update restart. Automatic session recovery also retains editing work and its file association, including whether changes are still unsaved.

Declining new license terms leaves the installed app and your saved data in place. A startup check does not authorize downloading or installing an update.

See [Updates](../App-Features/Updates.md) for the complete flow and [Editors And Saving](../App-Features/Editors-And-Saving.md) for document saving and recovery.
